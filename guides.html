Bookkeeping Basics

The Small Business Guide to Credit (Udhar) Management

Maintaining healthy cash flow is the biggest challenge for retail shopkeepers and freelancers. Extending credit (Jama/Udhar) helps build long-term customer relationships, but unrecorded credit leads to revenue leakage. Using a digital Khata helps maintain transparent records, automatic balance tracking, and instant PDF statement generation for customers.

Tax & Compliance

Understanding GST Rates and Reverse Calculation

In India, the Goods and Services Tax (GST) is split into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) for intra-state transactions, and IGST for inter-state business. When pricing goods inclusive of GST, reverse tax calculations ensure that you accurately report the true taxable value and input tax credit on your monthly returns.

Digital Tools

Why Digital Bookkeeping Outperforms Paper Ledgers

Traditional paper bahi-khatas are prone to physical damage, miscalculated totals, and loss over time. Cloud-synced ledgers secure records against device loss, allow multiple company management under a single login, and enable one-click exports to CSV and Excel for tax filing.
