Small Business Accounting & GST Guides

Essential financial record-keeping guides for Indian retail merchants and traders.

How to Maintain a Daily Cash Book (Khata Bahi)

A daily cash book tracks all incoming payments (cash-in / Jama) and outgoing expenses (cash-out / Udhar). For Kirana shops and retail businesses, keeping real-time records prevents payment defaults and maintains accurate working capital balances.

Understanding CGST, SGST, and IGST Slabs

Under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework in India, intrastate transactions split the applicable tax evenly between CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For example, an 18% GST invoice is split into 9% CGST and 9% SGST on taxable goods.

Difference Between a Tax Invoice and a Payment Voucher

A Tax Invoice is issued upon the sale or delivery of goods and contains item tax breakdowns, HSN codes, and GST rates. A Payment Voucher or Cash Receipt records simple fund transfers between parties without creating additional tax liability.
