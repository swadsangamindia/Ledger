Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Khata Bahi ("we", "our", or "us") operates the digital bookkeeping and ledger calculation platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal and financial calculation data is handled, stored, and protected.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver cloud sync services:

  • Authentication Data: When you log in via Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and unique user ID.
  • Bookkeeping Records: Transaction entries, customer ledger records, amounts, and dates are synced securely to your private cloud storage via Google Firebase.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website:

  • Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Data Security

Your financial records are protected by database security rules. We do not sell, trade, or distribute your private business entries or customer contact information to third parties.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us via email at: support@swadsangamindia.github.io.
