Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Khata Bahi, accessible from swadsangamindia.github.io, your privacy is important to us. This document outlines how data is handled.

1. Data Storage

All transaction ledgers, customer credit entries, and Gullak savings entries are stored locally on your device (localStorage) or synced securely to Google Firebase Firestore under your authenticated account. We do not sell or distribute your financial ledgers.

2. Cookies and Third-Party Services

We use third-party services including Google Firebase and Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites.

3. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: swadsangamindia@gmail.com
