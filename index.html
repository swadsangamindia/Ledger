About The Platform

Empowering Indian Merchants & Freelancers with Smart Digital Bookkeeping

Khata Bahi is an all-in-one digital accounting and multi-business ledger solution created for retail shopkeepers, Kirana owners, wholesalers, contractors, and independent service providers across India. Traditional paper accounts (Bahi-Khata) often lead to arithmetic mistakes, lost records, and uncollected debt (Udhar). Khata Bahi simplifies this with real-time balance calculations, automated GST breakdown, late payment interest computation, and multi-company profile switching.

Why Modern Businesses Choose Khata Bahi

⚡ Accurate Credit (Udhar) Tracking

Instantly record Jama (Credit) and Udhar (Debit) entries with date stamps to avoid balance discrepancies and track customer credit cycles.

🏢 Multi-Company & Shop Management

Manage distinct storefronts, branches, or side ventures under a single authenticated dashboard without mixing balances or invoices.

📊 GST & Late-Fee Financial Calculators

Calculate split GST rates (CGST/SGST/IGST) and determine compound interest on overdue client balances in seconds.

📄 Client-Side PDF & Excel Utility

Export statement sheets to CSV/Excel and compile customer bill receipts into PDF documents directly within your browser.

How to Use Khata Bahi in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Create / Select Your Business: Use the top selector to assign entries to your specific shop or enterprise profile.
  2. Log Entries in Real Time: Click "+ Add Entry" to input daily transactions with party name, amount, and payment status.
  3. Generate One-Click Statements: Use the export tools to download structured CSV reports for tax audits or share balances via customer summaries.
